FAST FACTS (1998 EDITION)
Capital City: Jakarta (on Java Island)
Total Islands: Over 17,500 islands! (6,000 inhabited)
National Motto: Bhinneka Tunggal Ika (Unity in Diversity)
Population: ~205 Million People
Currency: Rupiah (IDR)

ㄱ Major Islands of the Archipelago

Indonesia is famous for its massive chain of tropical islands. The 5 primary main islands are:

  • Sumatra: Home to famous Lake Toba, volcanoes, and wild orangutans!
  • Java: The most populated island, where the capital Jakarta and historic temples of Borobudur are located!
  • Kalimantan (Borneo): Dense, lush rain forests full of rare exotic wildlife!
  • Sulawesi: Uniquely shaped island known for brilliant diving reefs and rich traditions!
  • Irian Jaya (Papua): Mysterious thick jungles and high mountains topped with snow!

Special Mention: BALI - The Island of the Gods! World famous for beautiful beaches, surfing, and traditional legong dance!

🍛 Delicious Indonesian Cuisine & Culture

Indonesian food uses rich spices, herbs, and chili peppers! Here are the top dishes you must try if you travel here:

  1. Nasi Goreng: Authentic Indonesian fried rice, often served with a fried egg on top!
  2. Satay (Sate): Grilled meat skewers served with mouth-watering peanut sauce!
  3. Rendang: Slow-cooked spicy beef coconut curry originating from the Minangkabau people!
  4. Gado-Gado: Fresh vegetable salad mixed with rich peanut dressing and crackers (krupuk)!
💡 Did You Know? The famous Batik cloth is handcrafted using hot wax and natural dyes to form intricate patterns! It is worn proudly for formal events!

🐄 Exotic Wildlife

Because of its tropical climate, Indonesia has unique animals found nowhere else on planet Earth:

🦎 Komodo Dragon
The largest living lizard species in the world! Found on Komodo Island!
🦠 Orangutan
Gentle red-haired apes inhabiting the high canopy of Sumatra & Borneo!
